For SHGs, NGOs & FPOs

Apply to join the federation

Acomada federates registered self-help groups, NGOs and farmer producer organisations across Asia and Africa. If your organisation makes textiles, pottery, jewellery or woodwork and is not currently selling to Western Europe, we would like to hear from you.

01

Registration check

We confirm your organisation's registration as an SHG, NGO or FPO in its country of operation.

02

Craft review

We review samples of your work against Acomada's craft and quality standards.

03

Price floor set

Your group sets its own minimum price before anything is listed.

04

Listed & paid

Approved pieces go live in the collection; payments are made on a fixed schedule, tracked and published.

Partnership enquiry

We reply to every enquiry within ten working days.
