How It Works

From workshop to your home

Four steps stand between a producer group and your door. Each one exists to protect the price the maker receives.

01

We partner directly

We work only with registered SHGs, NGOs and FPOs — never through intermediary trading companies. Every partner group is a federation member, not a supplier.

02

Fair price is set first

Before a single item is listed, the producer group sets its own price floor. Acomada adds a fixed margin to cover verification, logistics and federation running costs — the maker's price is never negotiated down.

03

Quality is verified

Every piece is checked against Acomada's craft standards — construction, finishing and material sourcing — before it reaches the collection.

04

You receive it, they're paid

83% of what you pay goes directly back to the producer group on a fixed payment schedule, tracked and published in Acomada's annual accounts.
