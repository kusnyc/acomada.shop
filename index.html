From the Journal

Meet the collective behind our best-selling throw

In the villages of West Bengal, the Nakshi Kantha Collective has spent three generations perfecting a running stitch that turns old saris into heirloom textiles. Today, 34 women earn a stable income stitching for buyers across Europe.

How It Works

From workshop to your home

01

We partner directly

We work only with registered SHGs, NGOs and FPOs — no middlemen between the maker and the federation.

02

Fair price is set first

Artisan groups set their own price floor before a single item is listed for sale.

03

Quality is verified

Every piece is checked against our craft standards before it reaches the collection.

04

You receive it, they're paid

83% of what you pay goes directly back to the producer group, tracked and published annually.

"Acomada didn't just give us a market. It gave forty women in our village a reason to keep the craft of our grandmothers alive."
